Capital Gains — All ETF & Equity Categories

Equity Delivery & Equity-Oriented ETFs (Nifty 50, Sensex, Sectoral) — Section 111A (STCG) and Section 112A (LTCG). Holding threshold: 12 months.
STCG @ 20% LTCG @ 12.5% (above ₹1.25L)
Gold & Silver ETFs (GoldBEES, SilverBEES, HDFCMFGETF, etc.) — Merged into one row as they carry identical charge structures (STT = 0% on both sides, Stamp 0.015% buy only, DP ₹13.5 sell). Classified as non-equity listed securities. Holding threshold: 12 months (same as equity). STCG (≤12 months): Added to income, taxed at slab rate — not the flat 20% that applies to equity STCG. LTCG (>12 months): Flat 12.5% with no ₹1.25L annual exemption (unlike Section 112A equity gains). No indexation benefit.

Business Income — F&O, Commodities & Currencies

Equity Futures & Options, Commodity Futures, and Currency Derivatives are classified as Non-Speculative Business Income under Section 43(5) of the Income Tax Act, regardless of whether the taxpayer is an individual or firm.
Tax Rate: Profits are added to total income and taxed at the applicable slab rate (for individuals) or flat corporate rates (for companies). No concessional rates apply. Surcharge and 4% Health & Education Cess are added on top.
Slab Rate (Non-Speculative)
Expenditure Deductibility: All transaction costs — brokerage, STT/CTT, SEBI fees, exchange turnover charges, GST on brokerage — are fully deductible as business expenses under Section 37(1), reducing taxable business income directly.
Budget 2026 Hike Impact: Futures STT raised to 0.05% on sell side; Options STT raised to 0.15% on premium value (sell side). These elevated STT rates are now a significant cost drag, particularly for high-frequency options sellers where the tax applies to the full premium received, not just the profit component.
Futures STT 0.05% ↑ Options STT 0.15% ↑
At 0% Profit / Negative Return: Total transaction friction + any premium decay creates an official Non-Speculative Business Loss, eligible to offset against any non-salary business income in the current year. Unabsorbed losses can be carried forward for 8 years and set off against any business income (including speculative), subject to ITR filing before due date.
Friction = Business Loss (offsettable)

Speculative Business Income — Equity Intraday

Classification: Equity intraday trading (same-day square-off of listed equities on a recognised exchange) is mandatorily classified as Speculative Business Income under Section 43(5), Income Tax Act 1961 — regardless of intent or frequency.
Charge Specifics: STT is levied at 0.025% on both buy and sell sides (vs 0.1% each for delivery). Brokerage is ₹20 or 0.03% per leg (whichever lower). No DP charge — shares are never credited to demat as the position is squared intraday.
Tax Rate: Speculative profits are taxed at the applicable slab rate. Surcharge and 4% Health & Education Cess apply. No concessional STCG/LTCG treatment available.
Slab Rate (Speculative)
Critical Loss Restriction: Speculative losses can only be set off against speculative income — not against F&O gains, salary, or any other head. Carry-forward period is 4 years (vs 8 years for non-speculative F&O losses), and only against future speculative profits.
Offset: Speculative income only Carry forward: 4 years
