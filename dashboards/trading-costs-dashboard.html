Business Income — F&O, Commodities & Currencies

Equity Futures & Options, Commodity Futures, and Currency Derivatives are classified as Non-Speculative Business Income under Section 43(5).
Tax Rate: Profits taxed at applicable slab rate. All transaction costs deductible as business expenses under Section 37(1).
Slab Rate (Non-Speculative)
Budget 2026 Hike: Futures STT raised to 0.05%; Options STT raised to 0.15% on premium value.
Futures STT 0.05% ↑Options STT 0.15% ↑
At 0% Profit: Friction creates Non-Speculative Business Loss, offsettable against non-salary business income. Carry-forward 8 years.
Friction = Business Loss (offsettable)

Speculative Business Income — Equity Intraday

Classification: Equity intraday trading is mandatorily classified as Speculative Business Income under Section 43(5) — regardless of intent or frequency.
Charge Specifics: STT is levied at 0.025% on both buy and sell sides. No DP charge — shares are never credited to demat.
Tax Rate: Speculative profits taxed at applicable slab rate. No STCG/LTCG treatment.
Slab Rate (Speculative)
Critical Loss Restriction: Speculative losses can only be set off against speculative income. Carry-forward period is 4 years (vs 8 for F&O).
Offset: Speculative income onlyCarry forward: 4 years
