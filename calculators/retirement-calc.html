Government Benefits & Schemes

India · FY 2026-27
🏦

National Pension System (NPS)

Tax deductions up to ₹2L/year (₹1.5L under 80C + ₹50K under 80CCD(1B)). Employer contribution of up to 10% of salary is fully deductible. 60% lump sum at maturity is tax-free; annuity portion taxed at slab rate. NPS Tier-II has no lock-in.

📋

EPF / EPS Benefits

EPF earns 8.25% p.a. (tax-free). EPS provides a defined monthly pension — capped at ₹7,500/mo. Full EPF corpus is tax-free at retirement after 5 years of continuous service. Partial withdrawal available for housing, education & medical needs.

🏛️

Public Provident Fund (PPF)

Sovereign-guaranteed 7.1% p.a. under EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) tax regime. Annual deposit limit ₹1.5L. Partial withdrawal from 7th year; full maturity at 15 years (extendable in 5-year blocks). Interest and proceeds 100% tax-exempt.

🏥

Senior Citizen Healthcare

₹50,000 deduction on health insurance premiums under Sec 80D for senior citizens. CGHS covers central govt. retirees. Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY provides up to ₹5L/year hospital cover. Additional ₹50K 80TTB deduction on interest income.

🏠

Reverse Mortgage (RML)

Convert home equity into monthly income without selling. Loan up to 60% of property value — disbursed as monthly payouts. No repayment during lifetime; settled from estate after death. Loan proceeds are not taxable as income.

💰

Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS)

Highest sovereign-backed yield at 8.2% p.a. Max deposit ₹30L per account. Quarterly payouts make it ideal for regular pension income. 5-year tenure with one extension. Interest taxable but ₹50K 80TTB deduction offsets tax for most retirees.
