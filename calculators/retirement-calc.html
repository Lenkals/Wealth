Government Benefits & Schemes

India · FY 2026-27
🏦

National Pension System (NPS)

Tax deductions up to ₹2L/year (₹1.5L under 80C + ₹50K under 80CCD(1B)). Employer contribution of up to 10% of salary is fully deductible. Annuity from NPS is taxed as income; lump sum withdrawal of 60% at maturity is tax-free.

📋

EPF / EPS Benefits

Employee Provident Fund earns 8.25% p.a. (tax-free). EPS provides a defined monthly pension (max ₹7,500/mo). Full EPF corpus is tax-free at retirement after 5 years of service. Partial withdrawal allowed for housing, education, and medical expenses.

🏛️

Public Provident Fund (PPF)

Guaranteed 7.1% p.a. (tax-free under EEE regime). Annual contribution limit of ₹1.5L. Partial withdrawal from year 7; full withdrawal at maturity (15 years). Interest and maturity proceeds are completely tax-exempt.

🏥

Senior Citizen Healthcare

₹50,000 deduction on health insurance premiums (80D) for senior citizens. CGHS covers central govt. retirees comprehensively. Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana provides ₹5L/year hospital cover for eligible families.

🏠

Reverse Mortgage (RML)

Convert your home equity into monthly income without selling the property. Loan amount up to 60% of property value. Repayment required only upon death or permanent relocation. No income tax on loan proceeds received.

💰

Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS)

Highest risk-free yield among government schemes at ~8.2% p.a. Max deposit ₹30L. Quarterly interest payouts — ideal for regular pension income. 5-year tenure with one extension available. Interest taxable, but covered by ₹50K 80TTB deduction.
