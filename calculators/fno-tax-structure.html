⚖️ Income Tax Implications for F&O Trading

  • Taxation Category: F&O trading gains are not classified as Capital Gains. Instead, they are treated as Profits and Gains from Business or Profession (PGBP), falling under Non-Speculative Business Income.
  • Slab Rates Apply: Net profits are added straight to your total annual taxable income (including salary, rent, etc.) and taxed collectively at your applicable individual income tax slab rates.
  • Business Expense Deductions: Since it is considered business revenue, you can deduct all operational expenditures to lower your tax liability. This includes all transactional charges shown above, infrastructure depreciation, internet, and charting platform subscriptions.
  • Loss Set-Off & Carry Forward: Net F&O losses incurred over a financial year can be offset against other active business incomes, rental incomes, or interest income (but cannot clear salary income). Unabsorbed losses can be carried forward for up to 8 consecutive years if returns are filed within deadlines.
